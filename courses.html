Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to memorize the whole Quran?
Starting as a beginner, most students complete full memorization in about 3 to 5 years with consistent daily classes and revision.
Do I need any prior knowledge to start?
No — the Noorani Qaida course is designed for complete beginners, including students who don't yet read Arabic.
Can I try a class before committing?
Yes. Every tutor profile lets you send a free trial request before you book any paid classes.
Are classes available for children?
Yes — many of our tutors specialize in structured, engaging classes for young learners.
